The three rules

Easy days are easy. If you can't talk in full sentences, slow down.

No two hard days in a row. If Tuesday moves, the week moves with it.

A missed run is gone. Don't double up. Mark it missed and carry on.

Ill above the neck: easy runs only. Below the neck or fever: rest, then a week of easy.
